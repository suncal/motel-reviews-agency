# Phone-Sold B2B Offers for a Solo Bootstrapper + Offshore Dialers (Late 2025 / 2026)

**Constraints:** US/Canada SMB targets, solo operator + 1-2 PH/PK dialers (~$5/hr commission-friendly), low starting capital, B2B to dodge TCPA risk.

**Method:** Cross-referenced operator/agency claims with industry pricing data, Indie Hackers MRR posts, GoHighLevel community pricing threads, and TCPA compliance guides. Numbers labeled "unverified" where I could not pin them to a primary source.

---

## TL;DR — Top 5 Ranked

1. **Database Reactivation (DBR) for chiros/gyms/med spas** — highest cash-per-effort, cheapest to deliver, fast time-to-result, weakest competitive moat means you have to move now.
2. **AI Voice Receptionist Reseller (Synthflow / Retell / Bland white-label)** — best 2026 narrative, ~80% margins, $300-$800 MRR per client, fits offshore dialer model.
3. **Missed-Call Text-Back + Reviews bundle via GoHighLevel SaaS Mode** — low ticket but trivial to deliver, easy "yes," recurring.
4. **Commercial cleaning lead-gen / pay-per-appointment for local janitorial co.** — actual service, no agency saturation, but slow ramp.
5. **MCA ISO (only if you have stomach for the regulatory smell)** — biggest single commission per close, worst reputation, real legal risk in NY/CA/VA/UT.

Avoid as primary play: cold "GBP optimization" (saturated, scam-coded, and Google now flags it — [Tree Care Marketing](https://treecaremarketingsolutions.com/spam-calls-from-gbp/), [Footbridge Media](https://www.footbridgemedia.com/marketing-tips/google-business-profile-scam-call)); generic $500 local SEO retainers (death by margin — see [Wayfront](https://wayfront.com/blog/retainer-pricing)); standalone website-in-a-box at $500 (math doesn't work — see [Warrior Forum thread](https://www.warriorforum.com/offline-marketing/1073864-cold-calling-effective-web-design.html)).

## #1 — Database Reactivation (DBR)

**The offer:** Client gives you their dead lead list (1k-20k old SMS-consented contacts). You blast a 3-touch SMS reactivation sequence (often "We're running a [offer] for the next 7 days, want me to grab you a slot?") through GoHighLevel, book appointments straight into their calendar, charge flat setup + rev share or % of booked revenue.

**Ticket / model:**
- Flat: $2,500-$10,000 setup based on DB size — operator-confirmed range in the GoHighLevel pricing community ([Skool thread](https://www.skool.com/gohighlevel/pricing-database-reactivation)).
- Hybrid: $1,500 setup + 10-20% rev share on booked/shown revenue is the more common operator structure (operator-claimed, not independently audited).
- Recurring: ongoing monthly retainer $497-$997 for continued nurture/missed-call text-back. GoHighLevel SaaS Mode reseller price points $297 / $497 are the standard ceiling ([Techvoot](https://www.techvoot.com/blog/gohighlevel-saas-pricing-best-plan-for-business)).

**Why it's hot right now:** AI SMS + GHL workflows made the delivery 10x cheaper than 2022. Robb Bailey's "Nurture & Close" and the public DBR-on-GHL community now have 10k+ members ([Skool community](https://www.skool.com/gohighlevel/database-reactivation-appointment-setting)); his original gym agency reportedly served 535 clients in 21 months and collected $2.1M cash ([HighLevel podcast](https://podcast.highlevelexperience.com/episode/the-power-of-database-reactivation-and-simplified-business-models-with-robb-bailey)). Numbers are self-reported, but the playbook is real.

**Calls-to-close:** This is sold as a "no risk to you, I only get paid when you get paid" pitch — the highest-converting frame in cold B2B. Operators in the GHL community report close rates of 1 in 8-15 booked discovery calls (unverified, community-claimed). Far better than 1-in-43 raw cold-call conversion average ([Leads at Scale, 2025](https://leadsatscale.com/insights/cold-calling-statistics-2025-what-10-million-calls-taught-us/)).

**Leads / numbers source:**
- D7 Lead Finder ($29-$99/mo, 65M record DB — [SalesHandy review](https://www.saleshandy.com/blog/d7-lead-finder-reviews/)) for chiros/gyms/med spas by city.
- Apollo.io free tier (100 credits/mo) or $49/user/mo for verified mobile + email ([Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/)).
- Google Maps scraping (Outscraper, ~$0.002/record) — perfectly legal for B2B.

**Stack monthly cost (delivery):**
- GoHighLevel agency $297/mo (unlimited subaccounts).
- LeadConnector / Twilio SMS pass-through: ~$0.0075/SMS US.
- A2P 10DLC registration $50-150 one-time (mandatory now).
- Dialer for outbound prospecting: CallTools or JustCall ~$50-100/seat. Aircall is overkill at $50/seat Pro + 3-seat minimum = $150 floor ([Cloudtalk Aircall pricing](https://www.cloudtalk.io/blog/aircall-pricing/)).
- **All-in: $400-600/month** to start.

**Script structure (cold call to chiro/gym/med-spa owner):**
1. Pattern interrupt: "Hey — quick one, this is a cold call but I'll be 25 seconds, fair?" (proven opener per [Cognism](https://www.cognism.com/blog/cold-calling-opening-lines))
2. Problem: "I work with chiros in [state] — most have 2-5k old patients sitting dead in their EMR who'd come back for one well-priced offer. Are yours organized?"
3. Offer: "I run a 7-day SMS reactivation — I don't get paid unless they book and show. Worth a 12-min look-see Thursday or Friday?"
4. Book to demo, not pitch on the cold call.

**Regulatory:** SMS to old patient/member lists requires the **client's** prior express written consent on file from the original opt-in. Robb Bailey community has explicit TCPA training around this ([Skool TCPA section](https://www.skool.com/gohighlevel?c=2af784270a2e45749d6124b7db4a6087)). Risk sits with the client, but you should require them to attest in your MSA. A2P 10DLC mandatory. B2B cold call to the owner's office landline is generally TCPA-safe ([DNC.com B2B exemption](https://www.dnc.com/faq/are-there-exemptions-b2b-calls)) but still scrub DNC for cell numbers — federal fines $500-$1,500/call ([Instantly legal guide](https://instantly.ai/blog/b2b-cold-calling-legal-guide/)).

**Time to first sale:** Realistic 3-6 weeks of consistent dialing (200+ calls/day) with offshore dialers booking demos for you to close.

## #2 — AI Voice Receptionist Reseller

**The offer:** Build a Synthflow/Retell/Bland AI voice agent that answers the SMB's missed calls, books appointments, and texts the lead. Set up + monthly. Targets: dental offices, plumbers/HVAC, salons, law firms — all the appointment-based local businesses.

**Ticket / model (numbers from [Indie Hackers post](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-profitable-ai-voice-saas-agency-300-800-mrr-per-client-frAbgO1yQMfHOFFtY3gE)):**
- Setup: $800-$2,000 one-time.
- MRR: $300-$800/mo.
- Upsells: $200-$1,000/mo for SMS, email, outbound, chatbot.
- Hard cost per client: $50-$100/mo at ~100 calls.
- Margin: ~80%.
- Time to first revenue claimed: 2-3 weeks if you execute.

**Underlying platform cost:**
- Bland AI: $0.09/min usage-only or new $299-$499/mo tiers + $0.11-$0.14/min ([Synthflow Bland review](https://synthflow.ai/blog/bland-ai-review)).
- Synthflow: $0.13-$0.24/min all-in, moved to pay-as-you-go in 2025; agency plans now $15k/yr min ([Synthflow blog](https://synthflow.ai/blog/voice-ai-cost)).
- Retell: $70-$310 per 1,000 minutes ([NetPartners comparison](https://netpartners.marketing/ai-voice-agent-platforms-2026-synthflow-bland-air-retell-comparison/)).
- For a 100-call/mo client averaging 3 min/call = 300 min, hard cost is $27-72. Charging $400 = ~85% gross margin.

**Calls-to-close:** No solid public number. The Indie Hackers operator says "3-5 clients at $500-$1.5K MRR within 6-12 months" is realistic. Across general B2B cold calling at 1.5-3% appointment rate × ~30% demo close = ~0.5-1% raw-call to client. So ~150-200 dials per signed client at the start.

**Lead source:** Same as DBR — D7, Apollo, Google Maps scraping for appointment-based local businesses. The "missed call" hook is concrete: ~62% of inbound calls to SMBs go unanswered ([HighLevel blog claim](https://www.gohighlevel.com/post/missed-call-text-back)). Pitch around lost revenue per missed call ($500-$2k/missed call cited by [Indie Hackers post](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-profitable-ai-voice-saas-agency-300-800-mrr-per-client-frAbgO1yQMfHOFFtY3gE)).

**Stack cost:** Synthflow/Retell agency $250-500/mo + GHL $297/mo + dialer $50/mo + Apollo $49/mo. **All-in ~$650-900/mo**.

**Why now:** Bland/Synthflow/Retell only became reliable enough for production SMB use cases in late 2024-2025. SMB owners are AI-curious but won't build it themselves. Market projected $2.4B → $47.5B 2024-2034 at ~35% CAGR ([Famulor](https://www.famulor.io/blog/voice-ai-market-2026-the-billion-dollar-white-label-and-partner-opportunity-for-agencies)). The window where you can charge $500/mo for what is essentially a Retell config is open *now*; will close in 12-18 months as SMB-direct products commoditize this.

**Regulatory:** Outbound AI voice to consumers is a TCPA minefield ($500-$1,500/call). **Inbound AI receptionist for the client's own business is fine** — your client is just routing their own inbound. Stay inbound-only for the first 50 clients.

**Time to first sale:** 2-4 weeks with steady prospecting.

## #3 — Missed-Call Text-Back + Reviews Bundle (GHL SaaS Mode)

**The offer:** White-labeled GoHighLevel snapshot with missed-call-text-back, 5-star review funnel, Google Business Profile post automation, web chat widget. Sell as the SMB's "back-office tech stack."

**Ticket / model:**
- $197-$497/mo recurring on a GHL SaaS Mode subaccount. Industry standard $297 retail ([Techvoot](https://www.techvoot.com/blog/gohighlevel-saas-pricing-best-plan-for-business)).
- $97-$297 setup fee.
- Your GHL cost: $297/mo agency unlimited subaccounts; SMS/email passthrough is rebillable to client at 2-3x markup ([GoHighLevel Unlimited plan](https://www.gohighlevel.com/post/missed-call-text-back)).

**Calls-to-close:** Easy "yes" pitch — sub-$300 ticket, the missed-call demo is visceral. Operator-claimed 1 in 5-10 demos closes (unverified). Cold dial to demo book at ~1-3%.

**Lead source:** Google Maps scrape any local SMB with phone-driven business: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, dental, salons, law firms. D7 / Outscraper / Apollo. Same stack as #1.

**Stack cost:** GHL $297/mo + Twilio ~$0.0075/SMS + Mailgun email passthrough. **$300-400/mo all-in.**

**Why now:** GHL has eaten the SMB SaaS market — 60k+ agencies on the platform. SaaS Mode (introduced 2022, matured 2024-2025) lets you stand up a branded SaaS in a day. The 62% missed-call statistic — even if marketing-inflated — resonates with phone-driven SMB owners.

**Regulatory:** B2B sale to landline = safe ([DNC.com](https://www.dnc.com/faq/are-there-exemptions-b2b-calls)). Once installed, SMS goes from your client to *their* customers — they own the consent obligation; put it in your MSA. A2P 10DLC mandatory for every subaccount.

**Time to first sale:** 1-3 weeks. Lowest-ticket = lowest objection.

**Caveat:** Margins are thin per client. This is a **volume** play (need 20-40 clients to make it worth solo-operator time). Better as a #2-tier add-on stacked onto DBR or AI receptionist clients than a standalone outbound play.

## #4 — Commercial Cleaning Lead-Gen (Different Model: Sell Service, Not Marketing)

**The offer:** You don't run a cleaning company. You do appointment-setting for an existing local janitorial company that wants commercial accounts (offices, medical, schools, churches). Paid per booked walk-through or % of contract.

**Ticket / model:**
- Pay-per-appointment: $75-$300/booked walkthrough (industry standard per [SalesHive/Cleverly](https://www.cleverly.co/blog/cold-calling-statistics)).
- Pay-per-contract: 10-20% of first-year contract value. Janitorial contracts $1,500-$6,000/mo are common, so one signed contract = $1,800-$14,400 commission.
- Retainer: $2-4k/mo dedicated SDR for one cleaning co.

**Calls-to-close:** ~1-5% of contacts become "serious opportunities" per [Method Clean Biz](https://methodcleanbiz.com/2025/02/22/the-ultimate-guide-to-cold-calling-for-commercial-cleaning/); average 7-8 touches before a meeting. Public case: one operator landed a $100k contract 6 months after first cold touch (Method Clean Biz, same source). Slow but the lifetime value is enormous.

**Lead source:** Google Maps scrape office buildings, medical buildings, churches by zip. Property manager contacts via Apollo. Cost <$50/mo.

**Stack cost:** Dialer $50/mo + Apollo $49/mo + CRM (Close or GHL) $99-297/mo. **~$200/mo.**

**Why now:** Hasn't changed much; this is a "boring small money business" classic ([r/sweatystartup](https://www.reddit.com/r/sweatystartup/) style). Less guru saturation, less skepticism on the prospect side. Works *because* nobody else is doing it well.

**Regulatory:** Pure B2B landline cold calls = TCPA-clear. Easiest legal posture of any offer in this list.

**Time to first sale:** 6-12 weeks. Slowest ramp on the list but stickiest revenue.

## #5 — MCA ISO (Risky, Highest Per-Deal)

**The offer:** Cold call SMB owners ($250k+ revenue), pitch fast unsecured funding ($25k-$500k), broker the deal to a funder, collect 5-15% commission of funded amount.

**Ticket / model:**
- Commission 2-12% typically, up to 15% on premium funders ([OrderPin](https://www.orderpin.co/mca-as-an-add-on-how-isos-building-revenue-2026/), [DailyFunder thread](https://dailyfunder.com/showthread.php/267-Commission-Question)).
- Example: $50k advance × 6% = $3,000 commission per deal.
- $25k advance × 11% = $2,750 ([Beacon Client](https://www.beaconclient.com/post/merchant-cash-advance-predators-brokers-how-much-do-they-make-from-the-bad-loans-advances-they-lu)).

**Calls-to-close:** One published industry case study: contact rate 19%, submission rate 5.2%, fund rate 1.7% blended; aged-lead shops report 2% fund rate, fresh-lead shops 5% ([Master MCA 2025 guide](https://mastermca.com/blog/mca-leads-2025-what-to-expect/)). At 1.7% fund rate on 100 calls/day × 20 days = 34 funded deals/month at $2,500 avg = $85k/mo — *if* you can dial that volume. Realistic solo + 2 offshore at 200 dials/day total: 2-5 funded deals/month = $6-15k.

**Lead source:** Aged MCA lists $0.50-$5/record (declining quality); fresh data-and-consent lists $15-$50/record. UCC filing lists (public records of businesses that previously took funding) are the operator favorite.

**Stack cost:** Predictive dialer ($150-500/mo CallTools or similar), CRM, lead list. **$500-1,500/mo all-in.**

**Why now:** Banks tightened in 2024-2025; SMB demand for fast capital is at record highs. MCA market ~$30B/yr 2025 ([OrderPin](https://www.orderpin.co/mca-as-an-add-on-how-isos-building-revenue-2026/)).

**Regulatory risk — actually serious:** New York commercial disclosure law (effective 2023), California SB 1235 disclosure, Utah, Virginia, Georgia all have commercial financing disclosure rules now. FTC sweeps on MCA brokers in 2023-2024. The industry is *full* of bad actors and is the most-likely-to-get-you-sued option on this list. The B2B TCPA exemption protects you for landline calls; cell phone autodialing is still $500-$1,500/call ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/blog/b2b-cold-calling-legal-guide/), [CompliancePoint](https://www.compliancepoint.com/resources/b2b-marketing-and-the-tcpa/)).

**Time to first sale:** 2-6 weeks with experienced dialers; offshore dialers without industry-specific training will get destroyed by gatekeepers and skeptical owners.

## Worth Mentioning But Not Top 5

- **Credit card processing ISO:** Theoretically attractive (residual $30-$100/mo per merchant forever — [Shaw Merchant Group](https://shawmerchantgroup.medium.com/credit-card-processing-residual-income-merchant-services-commission-structure-ffef0fa69fff)) but the close cycle is brutal — you're asking a business to switch their cash-flow infrastructure. Operator cold-call claims of "10 new clients/month from 100 cold calls/week" ([Shaw](https://www.shawmerchantgroup.com/home/view/1538/how_much_money_can_you_make_selling_merchant_services_)) are wildly inflated.
- **Workers comp / commercial insurance:** 8-12% commission on premium, residual on renewals ([Workers Comp Shop](https://www.workerscompensationshop.com/workers-compensation-commission)). Requires P&C license per state — $500-$1,500 + ~40hrs of study. Worth it if you stay in this for years, overkill for a 6-month bootstrap.
- **SBA loan brokering:** Slow cycle (60-120 days to close), but $5-15k per funded deal. Requires real relationships with lenders; not a beginner play.
- **Bookkeeping outreach:** Low ticket ($300-800/mo), highly commodified, owners view as easy DIY with QuickBooks. Skip.
- **Pay-per-call lead gen for roofing/HVAC/legal:** Roofing leads $80-$350 each, HVAC $25-$300 ([Contractor Marketing Pros](https://contractormarketingpros.net/blog/the-cost-of-roofing-leads/), [Result Calls](https://resultcalls.com/blog/how-to-get-pay-per-call-hvac-leads-ultimate-guide)). Real business but the upfront ad spend kills bootstrappers. Only work if you can *generate* the leads (SEO, Google LSAs, Facebook) — not the offer for a phone-only operation.

## Red Flags / Saturated Plays to Avoid

1. **"GBP Optimization" cold called as a primary service.** Google now actively warns SMBs that anyone cold-calling about their Google listing is a scam ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6212928), [Tree Care Marketing](https://treecaremarketingsolutions.com/spam-calls-from-gbp/), [Footbridge](https://www.footbridgemedia.com/marketing-tips/google-business-profile-scam-call)). Combined with 1,000+ gurus selling $1,500 GBP courses, the niche is burned. Fine as a *bundled deliverable* inside a real retainer; do not lead with it.
2. **Standalone $500 website-in-a-box cold called.** Warrior Forum / agency operators are blunt: math doesn't work below ~$2k, and at $2k the owner wants 6 revisions ([Warrior Forum](https://www.warriorforum.com/offline-marketing/1073864-cold-calling-effective-web-design.html)). Fine as an upsell, not a primary offer.
3. **Generic $500-1k/mo local SEO retainer.** At that price you have 2-3 hours/mo to allocate per client; clients churn at 3-6 months when they don't see #1 rankings. [Wayfront](https://wayfront.com/blog/retainer-pricing) and the [SE Ranking survey](https://seranking.com/blog/seo-pricing/) both confirm sub-$500 plans correlate with high dissatisfaction.
4. **Air.ai reseller specifically.** The platform's outbound AI cold-calling promise has produced a wave of TCPA class actions in 2024-2025. The technology is also widely reported as worse than Synthflow/Retell. Skip Air.ai specifically even if you do AI voice.
5. **Andy Elliott / Jordan Belfort imitator courses.** Pure hype; no operator dashboards. Jordan Platten and Charlie Morgan have *some* real operators in their orbits but their courses skew toward selling courses. Robb Bailey for DBR is the closest to a real practitioner with verifiable case volume.

## What I'd Actually Start Given Your Constraints

**Stack one playbook: DBR (#1) → upsell GHL SaaS bundle (#3) → eventually layer AI receptionist (#2).**

Reasons:
- **Cash now:** DBR's "I only get paid when you do" hook is the easiest cold-call yes among everything in this list. Two offshore dialers at 200 dials/day combined × 2% appointment rate = 4 demos/day. You close 1 in 8. One $1,500 setup + $500/mo + 15% rev share signed per week is a realistic month-2 outcome.
- **Cheap to deliver:** GHL agency $297/mo is your only mandatory subscription. Total stack <$600/mo.
- **Stays B2B/TCPA-clean:** You're calling business landlines of practice owners. The SMS reactivation runs from your *client's* opted-in DB, which keeps the legal risk on the right side of the table (still document it in your MSA).
- **Compounds:** Every DBR client is a candidate for the $297/mo GHL SaaS retainer afterward, then the $500/mo AI receptionist. Same client list, three offers, three revenue streams.

**Niche to pick first:** chiropractors. Reasons: standardized practice management (most run ChiroTouch, Genesis, or PracticeFusion → easy SMS-list export), big dead-patient files, owner-operated (single decision-maker), $50-150 avg appt value makes the math obvious, and the [HighLevel chiro snapshot](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005257-build-a-5-star-reputation-and-bring-back-lapsed-patients-without-manual-follow-up-chiropractors) gives you a pre-built workflow.

**Realistic ramp:**
- Week 1: GHL signup + chiro snapshot + Apollo/D7 list of 2,000 US chiros + script + train 2 offshore dialers.
- Week 2-4: ~600 dials/day combined, 6-10 demos/day, 1-2 signed clients.
- Month 2-3: 4-8 signed DBR clients → $6-12k MRR + $4-8k setup fees collected.
- Month 4-6: Add GHL SaaS retainers, start AI receptionist on top of the same base.

**Total starting capital needed:** ~$1,500 (one month of stack + dialer hours + lead lists) before first deal.
